Welcome to Space Exploration

Space exploration is the discovery and study of outer space using spacecraft, satellites, and telescopes. Humans have explored the Moon, sent robotic missions to Mars, and continue to study distant planets and galaxies.

Why Explore Space?

  • Understand the universe
  • Search for life on other planets
  • Develop new technologies
  • Protect Earth from asteroids
  • Prepare for future human colonies
